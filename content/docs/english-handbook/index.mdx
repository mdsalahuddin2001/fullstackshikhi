## Where to start

1. Open the communication handbook and read **Your starting point**.
2. Complete the diagnostic and keep your first writing sample and speaking recording.
3. Follow the 12-week route, studying for about 45 minutes a day, six days a week.
4. Learn five vocabulary entries at a time. Create a true sentence with each one, then recall it without looking.
5. Repeat the speaking and listening tasks and check your answers before moving on.

## What to expect

These books provide a core self-study course. Reading alone does not produce fluency: speak aloud, listen, retrieve phrases, and repeat tasks. For the included listening scripts, use a device's English read-aloud feature or ask a partner to read them while you keep the text hidden.

A single written message cannot establish your overall proficiency level. Use the diagnostic and progress rubric to identify what you need to practise. The 12-week plan is a suggested schedule, not a guaranteed time to fluency.
