# 01 | Starting and maintaining conversations

by the way | প্রসঙ্গক্রমে / কথায় কথায় | introduce a related or new topic | By the way, are you free tomorrow?

fortunately | সৌভাগ্যক্রমে | introduce a fortunate fact | Fortunately, someone found my wallet.
unfortunately | দুর্ভাগ্যবশত | introduce an unfortunate fact | Unfortunately, we missed the last bus.
apparently | শুনে বা দেখে যা মনে হচ্ছে | information not presented as certain firsthand knowledge | Apparently, the shop has moved to a new location.
turn out | শেষ পর্যন্ত কোনো রকম হওয়া | turn out well / turn out to be | The trip turned out to be easier than I expected.
end up | শেষ পর্যন্ত কোনো অবস্থায় পৌঁছানো | end up + -ing / somewhere | We ended up walking home.
used to | আগে নিয়মিত করতাম, এখন করি না | used to + base verb | I used to study late at night.

in the end | শেষ পর্যন্ত / চূড়ান্ত ফল হিসেবে | introduce the final outcome | In the end, we decided to stay home.
learn a lesson | অভিজ্ঞতা থেকে শিক্ষা নেওয়া | learn a lesson from something | I learned an important lesson from that mistake.

# 19 | Polite and flexible responses
Would you mind…? | আপনি কি আপত্তি করবেন…? | Would you mind + -ing? | Would you mind closing the window?
I'd appreciate it if… | …করলে কৃতজ্ঞ থাকব | a polite request, often in writing | I'd appreciate it if you could reply by Friday.
I'm afraid… | দুঃখের সঙ্গে জানাচ্ছি… | soften an unwelcome message; not always fear | I'm afraid I can't attend tomorrow.
I'm not sure… | আমি নিশ্চিত নই… | express uncertainty | I'm not sure whether that's the latest version.
That works for me. | সেটা আমার জন্য সুবিধাজনক। | accept a suggested arrangement | Thursday at four? That works for me.
It's up to you. | সিদ্ধান্ত আপনার। | give the other person the choice | We can walk or take a bus. It's up to you.
No worries. | সমস্যা নেই / চিন্তা করবেন না। | informal response to an apology or concern | No worries. We can try again tomorrow.
Take your time. | তাড়াহুড়ো না করে সময় নিন। | allow someone time | Take your time. I'm not in a hurry.
Let me check. | আমাকে দেখে নিতে দিন। | pause to verify information | Let me check whether that time is available.
I see your point. | আপনার বক্তব্য বুঝতে পারছি। | acknowledge an argument, not necessarily agree | I see your point, but I'm concerned about the cost.
Could you be more specific? | আরেকটু নির্দিষ্ট করে বলবেন? | ask for precise details | Could you be more specific about the problem?
Sorry, I meant… | দুঃখিত, আমি বোঝাতে চেয়েছিলাম… | correct your own words | Sorry, I meant Thursday, not Tuesday.
# 20 | Linking and qualifying your ideas
although | যদিও | although + clause | Although I was tired, I finished the task.
however | তবে / তা সত্ত্বেও | connect contrasting statements | It's expensive. However, it may last longer.
therefore | তাই / সেই কারণে | a more formal result connector | The road is closed. Therefore, we need another route.
instead of | এর পরিবর্তে | instead of + noun / -ing | Let's walk instead of taking a taxi.
unless | যদি না | unless + clause | I'll come unless I have to work late.
as long as | যদি শর্তটি পূরণ হয় | as long as + clause | You can borrow it as long as you return it tomorrow.
even though | যদিও / তা সত্ত্বেও | emphasize a real contrast | I went out even though it was raining.
for example | উদাহরণস্বরূপ | introduce an example | Try a short task, for example, ordering a meal.
in other words | অন্যভাবে বললে | restate the same idea | We need more time. In other words, we can't finish today.
to some extent | কিছুটা / কিছু পরিমাণে | limit how strongly you agree or claim | I agree with you to some extent.
in general | সাধারণভাবে | describe what is usually true | In general, I prefer studying alone.
as a result | ফলস্বরূপ | introduce a consequence | The bus broke down. As a result, we arrived late.
